## Pitfalls comparables met

Hand items to other libraries: Bridges drop data (metadata, attributes, modes) and break when the wrapped library changes. gocloud's `Bucket` implements Go's `io/fs.FS`; cloudpathlib's `fspath` is a read-only cache copy.
