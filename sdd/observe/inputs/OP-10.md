## Pitfalls comparables met

Delete item: A missing key succeeds on S3 and in OpenDAL (even under a condition), and errors in object_store's local, GCP and Azure stores, gocloud, Azure and GCS; Hadoop returns False. Bulk limits are 1000, 256 and 100 keys per request, with partial failures. Versioned buckets insert delete markers. Observed on MinIO, not AWS: deleting a missing key gives 204, and a batch delete lists it as deleted.
