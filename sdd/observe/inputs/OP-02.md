## Pitfalls comparables met

Read at offsets: Ranges past the end were fixed separately in five libraries. Azure has no suffix range; S3 serves one range per request. Hadoop's vectored read may mix old and new data if the item changes. Observed on MinIO, not AWS: a range starting at or past the size gives 416, an end past the size is clamped, a suffix longer than the object returns all of it.
