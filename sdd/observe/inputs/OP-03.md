## Pitfalls comparables met

Write: Visible only on close, and close can fail. On an exception, pyarrow commits the partial object, cloudpathlib uploads it and smart_open aborts (observed). Multipart has a 5 MiB part floor and at most 10,000 parts. Empty items take a separate path in several libraries. S3 adds a CRC64NVME checksum when none is supplied, and "AWS SDKs do not automatically calculate MD5 checksums".
