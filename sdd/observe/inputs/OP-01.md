## Decisions comparables made once for every operation

Q4, decided once for every operation: Error taxonomy: object_store `Error` enum
and OpenDAL `ErrorKind`, each with NotFound, AlreadyExists, a precondition kind
and an unsupported kind; gocloud `ErrorCode`; fsspec and pyarrow builtins.

Q4, decided once for every operation: Retries: object_store built in, retrying a
timeout only if the request is idempotent; OpenDAL opt-in, temporary errors
only; s3fs an allow-list; gocloud not portable (inference); GCS retries deletes
by default.

Q4, decided once for every operation: Timeouts: per instance or per call:
object_store 30 s, OpenDAL 60 s (opt-in), botocore 60 s, Azure 20 s to connect
and 60 s to read, GCS 60 s; gocloud per call.

Q4, decided once for every operation: Consistency stated: atomic put
(object_store); strong read-after-write including list (S3, Azure, GCS);
gocloud's list may miss recent writes.

Q4, decided once for every operation: Closing: gocloud `Bucket.Close`; vendor
`close()`; OpenDAL `Operator`, fsspec and pyarrow have none; object_store relies
on Drop (inference).

bindings, sync and async parity in bindings, retry coupled to idempotency,
