## Pitfalls comparables met

List children: Directory markers changed policy across Hadoop releases. A missing folder is an error in Hadoop, optional in pyarrow and tolerated in OpenDAL. Ordering is not guaranteed in object_store, differs on S3 directory buckets and Azure HNS, and is lexicographic in gocloud. Pages can be short or empty; listing caches go stale.

## Findings in the research's results

Q2, the cut, list, walk, match (OP-06 to OP-08): The least agreed cut. gocloud
has one prefix-and-delimiter listing; object_store separates a recursive listing
from a delimited one; pyarrow merges inspect, list and walk into one call;
fsspec and cloudpathlib keep separate calls. Depth and pattern are emulated
client-side at O(subtree) cost (inference); only GCS matches a glob server-side.
