## Pitfalls comparables met

Conditional write: Conflicts surface as 412, 409 or 404 (a concurrent delete). A delete marker counts as absent. A local filesystem cannot do "if unchanged" (object_store #423); "if absent" maps to `O_EXCL`. object_store separates AlreadyExists from a precondition failure. OpenDAL forbids stat-then-mutate emulation and evaluates staged writes at the final commit, leaving the old item on failure.
