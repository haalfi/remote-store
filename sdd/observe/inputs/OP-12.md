## Pitfalls comparables met

Move or rename: Copy plus delete is neither atomic nor cheap. Atomic only on some bucket types: Azure HNS, GCS `move_blob` within a bucket, S3 `RenameObject` on Express One Zone. SFTP v3 rename fails if the target exists. Same-path moves differ; GCS charges early deletion on a copy-based move.
